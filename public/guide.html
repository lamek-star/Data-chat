Main pages

Home/Messages is for contacts and conversation. Community Portal is for groups and join requests. Market Rates and Reports are Pro. Records manages transactions. Settings manages the profile, security, backups, subscription, privacy, and sign-out.

Contacts and live messaging

Label/buttonWhat it does
My contact QRDisplays the account’s shareable contact QR and text record.
Add contactOpens camera scanning, QR-image import, and contact-text import. Only explicitly added contacts are shown.
Scan with cameraUses the native Android/iOS camera. Allow Camera permission. Choose QR image is the fallback.
Message field / SendWrites to cloud storage and immediately sends through Supabase Realtime to the other online user.
Attach transactionSends transaction details together with a QR representation.
Add transactionImports a received transaction once. An existing transaction ID produces an already-exists notice.
Voice messageRecords and sends audio for Pro members after microphone permission is granted.
Report / BlockReports inappropriate activity or prevents further contact messages.

Both users need internet or Wi-Fi. Messages persist in the cloud and remain available in later conversations.

Transactions and QR cash handoff

Label/buttonWhat it does
New transactionCreates a record with receiver, amount, currency (including AED), and transaction details.
Generate handoffCreates a unique six-digit private security key plus QR/text handoff data containing the intended receiver.
Share / Copy / DownloadSends the transaction QR to a contact or exports its handoff text.
Scan handoff QRReads a sender’s QR using the camera or an image.
Confirm / Release cashMatches transaction ID, receiver, and private key before completion.
Import / ExportMoves supported record files; duplicate transaction IDs are skipped.
Security: treat the six-digit handoff key like cash. Share it only with the intended receiver and never in a public community.

Communities, rates, and reports

Users may create groups from their contacts, invite contacts when authorized, discover approved communities, and request membership. The group owner approves or rejects requests. Only the administrator creates root communities and defines their location, hierarchy, purpose, and permissions.

Pro members can open market rates and financial reports, filter by market/country and asset (currency, crypto, gold, or silver), publish a named contact offer, and use Direct message to discuss an order. Market values are informational and should be confirmed independently.

Pro, payment, backup, and biometrics

Label/buttonWhat it does
Pay by cardCreates a Stripe Checkout session for the signed-in user.
Admin cash code / Apply codeRedeems a strong one-use cloud code for Pro. It is not used for registration or login. Reuse is rejected and the administrator sees who redeemed it.
Backup storageOffers phone download and configured cloud choices. Managed encrypted backup is a Pro option.
Biometric accessIn Settings → Device security, protects the active mobile session with fingerprint, face, or device PIN and locks after backgrounding.
Payment status: the currently configured Stripe checkout is in test mode. Live Stripe credentials, live Price/payment link, and a verified webhook are required before accepting real money.

Administrator portal

Open the administrator portal. The fixed username is datachat-harmony. During first local setup, choose a unique password of at least 12 characters and keep it outside the APK.

Label/buttonWhat it does
AccountsShows allowed metadata such as name, email, plan, status, and registration information.
Generate Pro cash codeCreates and copies a strong, cloud-backed, one-use Pro code.
Access codesShows used/unused status and the username/account that redeemed a used code.
ConfigurationStores shared non-secret service settings. Real secrets belong in Cloudflare or Supabase secret storage.
Root communitiesCreates official location/hierarchy groups and their purpose and permissions.
Recovery backupsManages authorized encrypted recovery-package metadata and files.

The administrator interface must not expose message bodies, private contacts, financial records, transaction keys, or decrypted backup contents.

Troubleshooting

OTP missing: check Spam/Promotions, confirm the address, use Resend, and verify Supabase SMTP/DNS.

QR camera unavailable: use the installed APK, allow Camera permission, update Google Play Services, or choose a saved QR image.

Message delay: confirm both phones have internet, neither user is blocked, and Supabase Realtime is enabled for direct_messages.

Admin code fails: generate it from the internet admin portal. Codes work once, and local/offline codes cannot activate another phone.

Biometric unavailable: first configure fingerprint, face, or device PIN in phone settings.
